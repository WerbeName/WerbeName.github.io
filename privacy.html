Privacy Notice

Last updated: 16 October 2026. This notice describes the current information website and the private development prototype. It must be reviewed and expanded before a public release.

1. Information website

This GitHub Pages website is static. Its source contains no analytics trackers, advertising scripts, sign-in form, cookies set by WerbeTracker, or functionality to upload VALORANT matches. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process technical access data according to its own terms and privacy statements. See GitHub's privacy statement.

2. Private development prototype

The separately hosted WerbeTracker prototype supports account registration through invitations and imports archived match and rank-change data from an authorized, locally configured collector. Such match records may contain Riot identifiers, names or tags, match participants, match events, timestamps, and performance statistics. The collector's API credential is intended to remain on the owner's computer.

Access to player profiles is controlled by account ownership and permissions. Team sharing and collaboration use explicit settings and server-side access restrictions. No publicly accessible player-statistics service is offered on this information website.

3. Purposes and retention

Prototype data is used to display post-match analytics, preserve match history, support player-directed training, and facilitate voluntary team planning. Some imported match records include other participants because they form part of the authorized match archive; those records are not made public by this website. The prototype includes account export and anonymization mechanisms, but archival match records may be retained after account anonymization for archive integrity. A complete public-service retention schedule has not yet been finalized.

4. Data sharing and external services

The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The private application uses a self-hosted backend and database. Riot Sign On, Overwolf production events, payment processing, advertising, and external AI services are not currently active public product integrations. Any future integrations will require a notice update and applicable user choices or approvals.

5. Questions and requests

For general inquiries, contact the project maintainer via GitHub. Do not disclose personally identifying match data, Riot credentials, or other sensitive information in public issues. Request an appropriate private contact channel before sharing such information.

Before a public release: The project operator must add a directly usable private contact address, verify the operator's identity and legal notices, clarify retention, hosting, legal bases and rights processes, and complete a privacy review appropriate to the jurisdictions served.
