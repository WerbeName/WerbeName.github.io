The intended experience is pre-match planning and post-match reflection. It does not include real-time competitive decisions or opponent scouting.

How the Riot API would be used

With player opt-in implemented through Riot Sign On, WerbeTracker intends to identify the consenting player and retrieve their authorized matchlist and match details for review and analysis. Aggregations such as agent trends, match outcomes, and coaching context would be derived after gameplay. Riot's API does not provide live match guidance or professional esports data for this product.

Reviewer note: This is a public visual flow for a product under development, not a publicly usable VALORANT stats service. The app will not open account-linked player history publicly until the required Riot approval, RSO onboarding, opt-in and privacy checks are complete. No official API credentials or private data are exposed on this site.
