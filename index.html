What WerbeTracker does

Analytics with a purpose.

Focused on post-match learning and voluntary collaboration, not in-match tactical advantage.

◫

Match archive

Review imported matches, results, agents, RR changes where available, and source-backed round details.

⌁

Performance insights

Explore trends by map, agent, session, and time. Missing data stays marked as unknown.

◇

Personalized planning

Get explainable, pre-match agent suggestions based on your authorized match history, not opponent scouting.

◎

Team sessions

Organize invited players, choose a coordinated group, and associate saved team plans with a session.

⌘

Strategy editor

Prepare versioned gameplans with roles, phases, positions, paths, and utility on a neutral diagram.

♧

Privacy controls

Account-based access, controlled profile sharing, private teams, and explicit permissions for collaboration.

Product workflow

From history to improvement.

These steps describe the currently developed local MVP. Availability for the public is subject to testing, legal review, and necessary approvals.

Import your archive

An authorized local collector supplies existing match data. Private credentials remain on the owner's computer.

Review your performance

Inspect your matches, trends, and evidence-based insights after gameplay. You decide what profile information to share.

Coordinate your team

Invite teammates, prepare strategies and gameplans, and review your efforts between matches.

Prototype status & contact

WerbeTracker is an independent project under active development. The public website is informational and does not provide a public application login. For project inquiries, visit the developer's GitHub profile. Do not publish account credentials or personal match archives in public GitHub issues.
